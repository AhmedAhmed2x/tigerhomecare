OUR SERVICES

In-home support for everyday life.

We can discuss the services that may fit your needs and care plan. Available services depend on eligibility, authorization, and program rules.

♙

Personal Care

Assistance with daily personal routines, such as bathing, grooming, and dressing, as authorized.

♜

Meal Preparation

Help preparing meals and maintaining regular mealtime routines.

⌂

Homemaking

Light housekeeping, laundry, and support with keeping the home comfortable.

●●●

Companionship

Conversation, shared activities, and friendly social support.

◷

Medication Reminders

Reminders to follow an established medication routine. This does not mean medication administration.

↗

Appointment Support

Assistance preparing for appointments and support while attending, when authorized.

Services are provided only when appropriate for the individual’s approved plan and applicable program requirements. Please contact us to confirm availability.
